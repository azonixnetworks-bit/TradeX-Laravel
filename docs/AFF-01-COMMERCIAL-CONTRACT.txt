FORCED PAYMENT EXCEPTIONS
Only the following approved external/mandatory exceptions can invalidate a cleared payment:
- PAYPAL_CHARGEBACK
- PAYPAL_PROCESSOR_REVERSAL
- DUPLICATE_PAYMENT_CORRECTION
- UNAUTHORIZED_PAYMENT
- TRADEX_BILLING_ERROR
- LEGAL_REQUIRED_REVERSAL

A forced exception creates a separate negative AffiliateCommissionAdjustment.
The original AffiliateCommission values remain preserved for audit.
